Getting there & settling in

Travel & Accommodation

Getting There

Golden Elm Manor is a 90-minute train ride from Grand Central Station, with direct routes to St. Augustine.

Complimentary shuttles will run every 30 minutes from the St. Augustine station to the venue, starting at 3:00 PM.

Parking is also available on site for those driving.

Where to Stay

We've reserved a block of rooms at two lovely spots — mention "Jenny & Jackson's wedding" when booking.

  • The Wildflower Inn Recommended

    Cosy & quaint, right in town. A 5-minute walk to the venue.

  • The Fox & Finch Manor

    A historic stay just minutes from the venue, perfect for families.
