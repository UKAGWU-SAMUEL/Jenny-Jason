Our Story

How it all began

Where the Wild Things Wed

We met at an art walk in Seattle — literally bumping into each other. Neither of us planned on staying long, but we ended up talking for hours. That was it.

We started hiking together, a lot. We both love the Pacific Northwest — the mountains, the coast, everything. We'd be out on trails, just hanging out, talking around a fire. It clicked. We were on the same wavelength.

One time, we were hiking and I complained about my boots and Jenny just stopped and started tying my laces for me. That's when I knew. It wasn't some grand gesture. It was just... us.
